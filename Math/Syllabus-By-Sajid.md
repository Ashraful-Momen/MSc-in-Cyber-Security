1. Descriptive Statistics & Data Summarisation: 
  Used to understand the basic features of the data and summarise key characteristics.

- Measures of Central Tendency: Mean, Median, and Mode.
- Measures of Variability/Spread: Variance, Standard Deviation, Range, and Interquartile Range (IQR).
- Data Visualisation: Histograms, scatter plots, box plots, and correlation matrices.

2. Probability Theory
  Provides the mathematical language for dealing with randomness and uncertainty.

- Basic Probability: Events, sample spaces, and probability rules (addition and multiplication rules).
- Conditional Probability: Dependent/independent events and the Chain Rule.
- Bayes' Theorem: Calculating posterior probabilities and the foundation of naive Bayes classifiers.

3. Random Variables & Probability Distributions 
  Used to model and quantify random phenomena in the real world.

- Random Variables: Discrete vs. Continuous random variables.
- Discrete Distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, and Geometric distributions.
- Continuous Distributions: Uniform, Exponential, and the all-important Normal (Gaussian) Distribution.
- Moments: Expected Value (E[X]), Skewness, and Kurtosis.

4. Inferential Statistics 
  Drawing conclusions and making predictions about a larger population based on a smaller sample.

- Sampling & Sampling Distributions: Random sampling, the Law of Large Numbers, and the Central Limit Theorem (CLT).
- Estimation: Point estimates and Confidence Intervals.
- Hypothesis Testing: Formulating null (H₀) and alternative (Ha) hypotheses.
- Key Statistical Tests: Z-tests, T-tests (Independent/Paired), ANOVA, and Chi-Square tests..

5. Advanced Modeling & Relationships
  Essential for feature selection, evaluation, and predictive model building.

- Covariance & Correlation: Understanding the relationship and direction of dependencies between two variables (e.g., Pearson, Spearman).
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE): Estimating the parameters of a probability distribution from observed data.
- Regression Analysis: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, and evaluating model fit (R², p-values).
